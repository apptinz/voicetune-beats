# Voice Tune — free beat library

Instrumental beats used by the **Voice Tune for Singing** Android app's free beat library.
The audio files are attached to the [`library-v1` release](../../releases/tag/library-v1):

- `bNNN.m4a` — full beat (AAC 160 kbps), `bNNN-preview.m4a` — 30-second preview
- `beats.json` — catalog read by the app (title, producer, genre, license, source page)
- `CREDITS.md` — every beat with its author, source and license

## Licenses

Every beat is either **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication) or **CC BY** (2.0 – 4.0). Each license was checked on
the beat's original page (Freesound or Wikimedia Commons) when the library was built. CC BY beats require
crediting the author; the app shows the credit line next to each beat and after export.

**Changes made:** beats were trimmed (leading/trailing silence), loudness-normalized to −14 LUFS with a short
fade-out, and re-encoded to AAC. Previews are 30-second excerpts. The originals are on the source pages listed in
`CREDITS.md`.

No beat in this library comes from a site whose terms forbid redistribution.

If you are an author and want a beat removed, please open an issue.
